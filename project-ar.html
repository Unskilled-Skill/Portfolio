AR You Ready — Story-Based First-Person Game

This project was created for the Planbureau voor de Leefomgeving (PBL) as part of the “Snelle Wereld” future scenario — a world where technology is fast, hyperconnected, and always demanding attention. The goal was to create an immersive storytelling experience that reflects how technology shapes daily life and human interaction in 2050.

AR You Ready is a first-person narrative game where the player experiences a day in the life of a journalist who relies on his AR glasses for work and communication. Throughout his day, he’s constantly interrupted by calls, notifications, and digital events — never fully present in the real world. As he tries to meet a friend, he’s pulled in all directions by his smart glasses, ultimately arriving late — a subtle commentary on the overload of connectivity in the “fast world.”

My Role & Contributions

I focused on the programming and world systems, helping bring the interactive story to life. My main contributions included:

  • Developing a dynamic objective system to guide the player through each scene.
  • Creating waypoint systems and event triggers to structure progression.
  • Building a progressive event-based system using interactable boxes to manage story flow.
  • Designing and building the world environment, ensuring smooth transitions and atmosphere.
  • Working on subtitles, dialogue timing, and voicing several characters to enhance immersion.

This project taught me how to blend narrative and system design, connecting gameplay mechanics with emotional storytelling. By focusing on both code and atmosphere, I helped shape a believable and engaging future world that encourages reflection on our relationship with technology.
